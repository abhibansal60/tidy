## Long post (X Premium)

I gave 110 YouTube channels to 8 AI models and asked the same four questions about each: how relevant it is to me, how substantive it looks, how clickbaity the titles are, and whether the evidence is enough to judge.

Jev finished all 110 in 9 seconds. Through their coding CLIs, Sonnet 5 and Opus 5 took 3 minutes 8 seconds, Sol 3m 48s, Haiku 4.5 7m 19s. Other people who tested Jev on a direct API measure 3 to 6 times faster, so read my 21 to 48 times as an upper bound.

At list prices the run cost Jev about $0.004. The others came to between $0.03 (Luna) and $1.11 (Astra). I didn't run Fable 5.1 and estimate it near $2. Run twice, Jev's scores moved 3 to 4 times less than Sonnet 5's.

Then I scored every model against my own keep or drop calls on 66 channels. All of them, Jev included, landed between 0.43 and 0.52. My Google watch history scored 0.69. The models can rate quality. They can't tell what I'll keep.

So Jev does the fast judging, code applies the thresholds, caps and budgets, and I approve every change. That's 11 unsubscribes so far. It also found 30 channels I watch often but don't follow and proposed 13. I turned them all down.
